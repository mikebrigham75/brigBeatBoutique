# Volume 1: Dirtchamber Style Test Run Mini-Mix (Audacity Blueprint)

**Style Influence:** Coldcut (*Journeys by DJ*) & Liam Howlett (*The Dirtchamber Sessions Volume One*)  
**Target Runtime:** ~5 Minutes  
**DAW:** Audacity (macOS Apple Silicon)

---

## Step 1: Audacity Workspace Setup

1. **Create Tracks:** Open Audacity on your MacBook and press `Cmd + Shift + N` four times to create **4 Stereo Tracks**:
   * `Track 1`: Main Deck A
   * `Track 2`: Main Deck B
   * `Track 3`: Breaks & Acapellas
   * `Track 4`: FX & Movie Stingers
2. **Sample Rate:** Set your project sample rate in the bottom-left corner to **44100 Hz**.
3. **Import Audio:** Drag your 6 chosen audio files onto the canvas so they are ready to chop.

---

## Step 2: The 6-Tune Edit Script (0:00 – 5:00)

| Cue | Time | Genre | Track / Sample | Approx. BPM | Audacity Mix Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | `0:00 – 0:45` | **Funk Intro** | **James Brown** – *Give It Up Or Turnit A Loose* | ~108 BPM | Slice out an 8-bar drum break (`Cmd + I`), copy-paste (`Cmd + C` / `Cmd + V`) for 16 bars. Layer a gritty movie quote/radio static on `Track 4` over bars 9–16 and duck drums with the Envelope Tool (`F2`). |
| **02** | `0:45 – 1:45` | **90s Indie** | **Primal Scream** – *Loaded* OR **The Stone Roses** – *Fools Gold* | ~100–112 BPM | Tempo-match the James Brown loop (`Effect > Pitch and Tempo > Change Tempo`) to match your Indie track. Line up the first Indie snare directly under the final funk snare on `Track 2`. |
| **03** | `1:45 – 2:45` | **Acid House** | **The Chemical Brothers** – *Goodbye* | ~130 BPM | Highlight the last 1.5s of the Indie track and apply `Effect > Pitch and Tempo > Sliding Stretch` (`-85%` tempo) for a turntable brake. Slam in the 303 acid line on `Track 1` as the brake stops. |
| **04** | `2:45 – 3:55` | **Big Beat / Rock (2 Tunes)** | **Fatboy Slim** – *The Rockafeller Skank* + **Fatboy Slim / The Rolling Stones** – *Satisfaction Skank* | ~132–153 BPM | Let *Goodbye* peak, then cut audio (`Cmd + I`) for 1 second of dead air. Drop the *"Right about now, the funk soul brother"* vocal hook on `Track 3` into the silence, slamming *Satisfaction Skank* underneath on `Track 2` before riding *The Rockafeller Skank* breakdown. |
| **05** | `3:55 – 5:00` | **90s Jungle Finale** | **Shy FX** – *Original Nuttah* OR **Goldie** – *Inner City Life* | ~168 BPM | Let *Rockafeller Skank* hit its speeding-up breakdown section—right at the peak, hard-cut into explosive 90s Amen breakbeats on `Track 1` to ride out the final minute. |

## Step 3: Quick Audacity Keyboard Shortcuts (macOS)

* **Split / Slice Clip:** `Cmd + I`
* **Duplicate Clip:** `Cmd + D`
* **Envelope Tool (Volume Curves):** `F2`
* **Selection Tool:** `F1`
* **Zoom In / Out:** `Cmd + 1` / `Cmd + 3`

---

## Step 4: Exporting Your Test Run

1. Go to `File > Export Audio`.
2. Choose **WAV (Microsoft) signed 16-bit PCM** (for lossless quality) or **MP3** at **320 kbps**.
3. Listen back on headphones to check beat alignment and transition punchiness.
